Meet Léa.

The personal assistant your parents deserve — and the peace of mind you've been looking for.

Léa is a voice-based AI companion that calls elderly parents every morning, keeps them company, helps them navigate technology, and keeps their adult children informed — quietly, reliably, every single day.

The Problem

Two people. Two very different worries.

The modern family faces a gap no product has fully closed: the adult child can't be there, and the elderly parent won't ask for help. Léa bridges both sides of that gap.

For the adult child

"Did Mum take her medication? Did she get home?"

You live across town or across the country. You call every day, but between those calls there are hours of silence. One missed call and the anxiety spikes. You carry a background dread you can't quite switch off.

For the parent

"I can manage perfectly well on my own."

She's right — she can. But modern smartphones weren't designed for her. WhatsApp, video calls, official letters from the Sécu — the digital world is full of friction that makes her quietly dependent on her children for things she'd rather handle herself.

The Léa value proposition
Features & Services

Everything Maman needs.
Everything you need to know she's fine.

Core Feature ☀️

Daily Morning Check-in Call

Léa calls every morning at an agreed time. She asks how Maman slept, follows up on something mentioned yesterday, reminds her about medications in conversation — never as a checklist. The call is 5–7 minutes of genuine, warm company. The adult child receives a clear summary by email or WhatsApp immediately after.

Walk Safety 🚶‍♀️

Safe Walks, Full Independence

When Maman goes out, a discreet protocol activates. You see her location on a simple map. Léa checks in if the walk runs long. You're notified when she leaves and when she's home. One button connects her to Léa if she feels lost.

For Maman 📱

Tech Concierge On Demand

One button, anytime. Léa helps send WhatsApp messages, make video calls, book a cab, order from the pharmacy, understand an official letter — all by voice, without navigating a single menu.

For the Family 📊

Daily Family Report & Dashboard

A humanly written summary arrives each morning: mood, medication, sleep, plans, anything noteworthy. The family dashboard shows trends over weeks — mood, adherence, activity — so you see the pattern, not just the day.

For Maman 💊

Medication Reminders

Woven into the morning conversation, not a mechanical alert. Léa asks. Maman confirms. Six months of adherence data logged — genuinely useful at medical appointments.

For the Family 🔔

Anomaly Alerts

Missed call, two days of low mood, unusual confusion, a mention of a fall — flagged immediately, with context. Not an alarm. A thoughtful notification that tells you what to do next.

For Maman 🛡️

Scam Protection

"EDF called and said they'll cut my electricity." Léa identifies the pattern, reassures Maman, and alerts the family. A trusted second opinion before anything goes wrong.

Later Phase 📖

Memory Keeper

Once a week, Léa asks about a memory from Maman's past. The answers are transcribed into a private family archive. Over months, an irreplaceable personal history — written in her own words.

For the Family 🏥

Post-Hospitalization Protocol

When Maman comes home from hospital — the highest-risk period — Léa increases call frequency and sends detailed daily updates for two weeks. The family has full visibility precisely when they need it most.

How It Works

Simple for Maman.
Powerful for you.

1

You fill in a short intake form

Tell us about your parent — her routines, her personality, her medications, her family. What makes her laugh. What to avoid. This becomes Léa's starting point.

2

Léa calls on Day 1 — just to say hello

No features. No instructions. A warm voice, a kind conversation, a quiet end. Maman decides whether she likes her. Most do, by day three.

3

Léa learns, week by week

Every call adds to Léa's memory. By week four she knows Maman's rhythms, her stories, her family. The relationship deepens — and so does the value.

4

You check the dashboard when you want to

Or just read the morning email. Or wait for the "elle va bien" notification that arrives after each call. You're in control of how much you see.

5

If something needs attention, you'll know

Léa escalates the moments that matter. Everything else, she handles quietly — so you can stop worrying between calls and simply be present when you're together.
